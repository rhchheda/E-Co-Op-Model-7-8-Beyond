Customer Insight Survey — Key Questions

The five questions every team's survey should validate, also tracked per-team on the Teams page:

  • Real problem? Is this a real problem people actually face?
  • Current solution? How is it being solved today?
  • Target market? Whose problem is it?
  • Customer interest? Are they interested in the proposed solution?
  • Willingness to pay? Would they actually pay for it?
